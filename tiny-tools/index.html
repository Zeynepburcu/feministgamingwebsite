Tiny Game Making Tools

Small, accessible tools for making games

A collection of free-to-use, mostly open-access game-making tools. Some are aimed at particular genres; some are open to forms of self-expression beyond games. Most aren't just about the functionality of making a game — they're full communities, with their own subculture, shared knowledge, and events, awaiting your contribution.

More on the Topic

  • A more comprehensive (if slightly outdated) collection of game-making tools: creatools.gameclassification.com
  • Nathalie Lawhead (alienmelon) has a wonderful blog post on these tools and why they matter for creatives.
  • Follow Game Poems Magazine — don't overthink it, and submit your tiny game!
  • Visit freegamedev.net for a community around open-source game-making tools.

Many of these communities host online game jams and Discord servers you can join.
