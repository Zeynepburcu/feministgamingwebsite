Fellowship Program

Supporting new voices in feminist game design

The Feminist Gaming Fellowship is designed to support and amplify emerging creators who are pushing the boundaries of inclusive representation in digital games. As part of our broader mission funded by the Creative Industries Fund NL, the fellowship provides resources, mentorship, and community to help fellows develop meaningful contributions to the field.

What the Fellowship Offers

🎨

Creative Support

Access to tools, software, and production support to develop inclusive game assets, prototypes, or design research within the project's framework.

🤝

Mentorship & Network

One-on-one guidance from our core team and advisors — including experts in feminist design, game studies, and digital culture — alongside a vibrant peer community across NL, TR, DE, and the UK.

📚

Open Access Library

Contribute directly to the Open Access Asset Library (OAL) — a living repository of diverse 3D characters, animations, and design tools that challenge dominant game aesthetics.

🌍

Visibility & Exhibition

Opportunities to present your work through the project's travelling exhibition program, publications, and public events to reach wider audiences and amplify feminist game practices.

Who Can Apply

The fellowship is open to game designers, 3D artists, animators, researchers, writers, and other creative practitioners who are committed to advancing inclusive and intersectional representation in games. We especially encourage applications from:

  • Emerging creators and independent practitioners
  • Individuals from underrepresented communities in the games industry
  • Researchers working at the intersection of games, gender, and culture
  • Artists and designers exploring alternative body types, identities, and narratives
  • Creators based in or connected to the Netherlands, Türkiye, Germany, or the UK

Prior professional game development experience is not required — we value diverse perspectives and interdisciplinary approaches.

Program Structure

Open Call & Selection

Applications are reviewed by the project's core team and advisors. We look for alignment with the project's feminist and intersectional values, creative vision, and potential for impact.

Onboarding & Community

Selected fellows join the project network and are paired with mentors. Fellows participate in workshops and design sessions alongside the broader community.

Creation & Collaboration

Fellows develop their projects with ongoing support — creating assets, tools, or research that contributes to the OAL and the wider ecosystem of inclusive game design.

Showcase & Publication

Fellows present their work through exhibitions, publications, and community events, ensuring their contributions reach both the games industry and broader cultural audiences.
