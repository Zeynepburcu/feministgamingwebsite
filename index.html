Transforming Representation

The Feminist Gaming Project is a collaborative, international initiative working to transform gender and intersectional representation in digital games. Supported by the Creative Industries Fund NL, we focus on tools, assets, and community action to reshape how games are made from the ground up.

Our Core Actions
  • Building an Open Access Asset Library (OAL) of inclusive, diverse 3D characters, animations, and tools.
  • Running workshops, game jams, and community events across NL, TR, DE and the UK.
  • Supporting creators through a Fellowship Program.
  • Co-producing travelling exhibitions and publications.

Open Access Asset Library

The OAL represents a collaborative form of feminist infrastructure: a living, distributed repository of inclusive 3D assets and design resources. It encourages alternative representations—such as diverse body types, ages, and abilities—and promotes intersectional perspectives across gender, race, and identity.
